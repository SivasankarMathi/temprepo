Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I reset my password?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions. We'll send you a reset link via email. Click the link and create a new password.

❓ How do I update my profile?

Go to Profile from the sidebar menu. You can edit your name, email, avatar, and other personal information. Don't forget to save your changes.

❓ Can I export my data?

Yes! Navigate to Settings → Data & Privacy and click "Export My Data". We'll prepare a file with all your information which you can download.

❓ How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account Management and scroll to "Danger Zone". Click "Delete Account" and follow the confirmation steps. This action is permanent.

❓ Is my data secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) and follow best practices for data security. Your data is stored securely and is never shared with third parties.

❓ What is your privacy policy?

You can find our full privacy policy in the footer of every page. We respect your privacy and only collect data necessary for providing our services.

❓ How do I upgrade my account?

Go to Settings → Billing and select your desired plan. Upgrades are instant and you'll have access to premium features immediately.

❓ Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. If you're not satisfied, contact our support team for a full refund.
